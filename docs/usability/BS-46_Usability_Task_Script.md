# BookSwap usability test: task script

BS-46, story P04-US12 · Prepared by Krishna · Sessions on Sat 28 Nov 2026 with six students who are not on the team.
Record every session in `BS-46_Usability_Recording_Sheet.xlsx`.

## Before each session (2 minutes)

- Open the live app on a phone or laptop, signed out, on the first page.
- Have a stopwatch and the recording sheet open.
- Sign the student in with a test account (Test Student 1, 2 or 3) only if they don't have a BookSwap account.

## What to say at the start

> Thanks for helping. We're testing the app, not you, so nothing you do is wrong.
> Please think aloud as you go: tell us what you're looking for and what you expect to happen.
> I can't help while you're doing a task, but you can stop a task at any time.
> It takes about 10 minutes.

## Tasks (read each one aloud, then start the stopwatch)

| Task | What we say to the student | Done when | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 Sign in | Sign in to BookSwap with your name and XIME email. | Lands on the book list | 60 s |
| T2 Find a book to buy | Find a Marketing book that is for sale and check its price. | Opens a Sell book in Marketing and reads the price | 90 s |
| T3 Request a swap | Request a book that is up for swap, offering one of your own books. | Sees "Request sent" with the offered book | 120 s |
| T4 List a book | List one of your books for lending, with a photo and its condition. | Sees the book on the list | 180 s |
| T5 Check a request | Find out whether your request from T3 is still pending. | Opens My Requests and reads Pending | 45 s |
| T6 Safe meeting rules | Find where you are allowed to meet to hand over a book. | Opens Help and names a handover point | 45 s |

T2 covers searching, T3 and T5 cover requesting, and T4 covers listing (acceptance criterion 3).

## While they work

- Stop the clock when the "Done when" point is reached, or after twice the target time.
- **Completed?** Yes (alone), With help (we had to point), or No.
- Write down every place they hesitate, tap the wrong thing, or say they're confused (criterion 4). Use their words.
- Count errors: wrong taps that took them away from the task, or wrong data entered.
- After each task ask: "On a scale of 1 to 5, how easy was that?" and record it.

## After the tasks (2 minutes)

1. What was the most confusing part?
2. Is there anything you expected to find but didn't?
3. Would you use BookSwap for your next course book? Why or why not?

## After all six sessions (BS-48, Chandana)

- The Summary tab works out completion rate, average time and ease per task.
- List every issue in the Findings tab with how many of the six students hit it and its severity.
- Pick the top three and add each to the Jira backlog as a work item with a clear summary and a link to the findings (criteria 2 and 5).

Test accounts for the sessions are reset afterwards: delete any test listings from My Listings.
